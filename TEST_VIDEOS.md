## 🚫 Videos That WON'T Work

These types of videos will show errors:

### Age-Restricted Videos
```
Error: "This video requires authentication (age-restricted or private)"
```
**Solution**: Try a non-age-restricted video

### Private Videos
```
Error: "Video is unavailable or private"
```
**Solution**: Only public videos work

### Members-Only Content
```
Error: "This video requires authentication"
```
**Solution**: Channel membership videos don't work

### Region-Locked Videos
```
Error: "Video not available in your region"
```
**Solution**: Use a VPN or try different video

## 🔍 Debugging Failed Downloads

### Step 1: Check the Video
Open in incognito browser - does it play without login?

### Step 2: Check the URL
- ✅ YouTube: `youtube.com/watch?v=...` or `youtu.be/...`
- ✅ Instagram: `instagram.com/p/...` or `instagram.com/reel/...`
- ❌ Other sites not supported

### Step 3: Check Error Message
The app tells you exactly what's wrong:
- "requires authentication" → Age-restricted
- "unavailable or private" → Not public
- "region" → Geographic block
- "copyright" → Taken down

### Step 4: Try Different Video
Use one from the guaranteed list above!

---

## 📝 Common Error Messages Explained

| Error Message | Meaning | Solution |
|--------------|---------|----------|
| "requires authentication" | Age-restricted or private | Try public video |
| "unavailable or private" | Video removed/private | Find different video |
| "not available in your region" | Geographic restriction | Use VPN or try different video |
| "copyright" | DMCA takedown | Video no longer available |
| "not from supported platform" | Invalid URL | Check URL format |
